Debit_Rencana

R24 (T=10, LP3) → tc → intensitas Mononobe → debit Rasional

Q = 0,886 m³/dtk
Rantai perhitungan
R24 = XT(T=10, LP3) = 163,236 mm to = 0,0195·(Lo/√S)^0,77 (Kirpich, atap) = 0,15 mnt td = Ld/(60·v) = 3,25 mnt tc = (to+td)/60 = 0,0568 jam I = (R24/24)·(24/tc)^⅓ (Mononobe) = 383,2 mm/jam Q = 0,278·C·I·A = 0,278·0,80·383,2·0,0104 = 0,886 m³/dtk (envelope) D = 900 mm · v = 1,39 m/dtk · kapasitas Manning 1,172 m³/dtk Va = Q·th = 0,886 × 3600 ≈ 3.190 m³ (th = 1 jam)
